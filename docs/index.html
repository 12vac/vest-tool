VestTool

VestTool is for active traders who manage open trades on the fly: move the stop, cut half, close, flip, while keeping one eye on the account limits and one on volatility to choose size and stop distance. It turns the revenge trade and the oversized position into something you see, at a glance, while it is still a thought.

The visible trade

  1. We reject the blind click. Every order has a price in dollars, and that price belongs on the chart, next to the candle, before the finger moves.
  2. Speed without sight is gambling. The active trader moves stops, takes partials, flips. The tool must be as fast as the hand, and the limits of the account must never leave the eye.
  3. Volatility decides size and distance, not mood. The same hours, seen from 1h down to 1m. When 1m burns red, the stop goes wider and the size goes smaller. Not the other way round.
  4. The revenge trade has a shape. After a loss the next order comes bigger and faster. Here it has a form: the drawdown left shrinks, the fail line walks towards the price, the daily loss left turns red. You see the step longer than the leg while you are taking it.
  5. Oversize is a number, not a feeling. Every size is a percentage of what the account can bear. The cap refuses what the hand asks for in anger.
  6. Consistency is structure, not willpower. Daily goal, daily loss left, drawdown left: the same bars every day, the same rules every trade.
  7. The machine writes the code; the trader brings the method. This tool was written with AI. The ideas, the limits and the judgment are human. What is worth sharing is the method, the imagination behind it, and the people who sharpen it.
  8. Open, or nothing. Every line readable, nothing phones home, nothing re-arms by itself. Trust is built in public.

Ideas, criticism and better methods are welcome: this is meant to become a place for traders who think.

What it does

Everything runs in your browser, on the Vest page you already use.

$

Accounts in dollars

Equity, balance, today's P&L from the start of the Vest day, open and total P&L, and the dollars left before each drawdown limit, for every account.

↕

Orders from the chart

Drag SL, TP, limit and stop orders to the price you want. Breakeven, BE+, partial close, close and flat with one click. Risk & targets sizes the order from your dollar risk, with up to four take profits.

!

Fail price

Lines at the price where the account would reach its drawdown limit, and where half the room is gone, counting your pending entries.

≋

Trailing stop and auto BE

Moves Vest's real stop behind the price, in points or dollars, with a start level and a minimum step.

⇉

Copier

Mirrors one account's orders onto your other accounts, 1:1 or scaled to their equity, including orders placed with Vest's own ticket.

◷

Chart extras

Countdown to the candle close on Vest's clock, last swing high and low with the range in points, a volatility heatmap from 1h to 1m over the same hours, average entry if your limits fill.

Built to be safe by default

The script can touch real money, so its defaults are the cautious ones.

  • Orders OFF after every reload. While off it can only close and cancel, never open.
  • Copier, trailing and auto BE always start switched off; nothing re-arms by itself.
  • Talks only to Vest. No analytics, no telemetry, no external libraries, no auto-update.
  • Tokens stay in memory. Never saved, never logged, never sent anywhere but Vest.
  • Order endpoints only. Withdrawals and account settings are blocked in the code.
  • Size cap. No entry order can exceed the % of the maximum size you set.
  • Open source. One readable file, no dependencies: read it or build it yourself.
  • FLATTEN ALL. One button closes every position on every account, even with orders off.

Install in five minutes

Full instructions, updating and troubleshooting are in the step-by-step guide.

  1. Install the Tampermonkey extension from your browser's extension store (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox). On Chrome and Edge, allow userscripts if Tampermonkey asks (Developer mode or "Allow User Scripts").
  2. Download vest-tool.user.js from the latest release.
  3. Tampermonkey → Create a new script, replace everything with the content of the file, Save.
  4. Open next.vestmarkets.com, log in, reload. The panel appears as a column on the right of the page, with Trade commands under it.
  5. Click any ? in the panel to learn what each part does. Switch Orders on only when you are ready, and start at minimum size.

Questions

Is this allowed by Vest?

The tool sends the same orders you could send by hand on Vest's page. Check Vest's terms for your account type yourself, especially for copying between accounts: the rules are theirs, and they can change.

Does it need my password or API key?

No. It uses the session you are already logged in with, inside the Vest page, and keeps it in memory only.

Why is there no automatic update?

So that new code never runs on your account without you choosing it. To update, install the new release over the old one.

Which browsers?

Desktop browsers with Tampermonkey: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox. Not mobile.

Something looks wrong. What do I do?

If in doubt press FLATTEN ALL. Then open Settings → Diagnostics, press "Copy diagnostics" and open an issue on GitHub with it. The diagnostics contain no tokens, ids or amounts.

Can you add feature X?

Open an issue with the idea. This is a one-person project done in spare time: bugs come first, and not every request will be built. Pull requests are welcome.

Bugs and ideas

VestTool is free and will stay free (GPL-3.0). Bugs and ideas go in the issues on GitHub.

Before you open an issue

Add the version (next to the panel title), your browser, what you did, what you expected, and the output of "Copy diagnostics". Never paste screenshots that show your account ids or tokens.
